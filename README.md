# Japanese Dark Fantasy — Unity Game

A 3D action-adventure game developed in **Unity** as a university project and potential bachelor's thesis project.

The game is set in a dark fantasy version of historical Japan, combining traditional Japanese environments and mythology with supernatural enemies inspired by **yokai** and Japanese folklore.

> **Project Status:** 🚧 In Progress

---

## 🎮 About the Game

The player takes the role of a warrior who begins their journey with minimal equipment and gradually develops their character by exploring the world, acquiring equipment, and fighting supernatural enemies.

The main focus of the project is to create a complete playable gameplay experience while experimenting with 3D game development, combat mechanics, enemy AI, character progression, and environmental design.

The visual direction is planned around a **stylized dark-fantasy interpretation of feudal Japan**, rather than strict historical realism.

## 🎨 Visual Style

The game will use a **stylized dark-fantasy art direction** inspired by traditional Japanese architecture, mythology, and samurai-era aesthetics.

The goal is to combine:

- Japanese historical aesthetics
- Dark fantasy
- Stylized 3D characters
- Atmospheric environments
- Supernatural creatures
- Cinematic lighting

The visual style is intentionally designed to remain achievable for a small-scale development project while maintaining a consistent artistic identity.
